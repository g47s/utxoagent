What if a name could become an agent?

Realms are more than colorful words. They are unique names registered through the Atomicals protocol on Bitcoin.

🌱

Every Realm has an identity

A Realm is a unique name anchored in Bitcoin through Atomicals. It can represent a person, a community, a project or an idea. Its ownership follows the protocol's rules, rather than a username controlled by a single website.

🤖

What if Realms could host agents?

If AVM becomes operational and the required tools are built, a Realm could potentially serve as the persistent identity of an autonomous software agent. Such agents might communicate, perform permitted tasks, manage assets and interact with other agents under defined rules and spending limits.

Built on Bitcoin. Designed to explore.

Bitcoin provides the underlying transaction record. UTXOs represent spendable outputs. Realms offer unique names. AVM could add programmable rules if its execution stack becomes operational. Together, these pieces inspire a new way to think about autonomous digital identities.
