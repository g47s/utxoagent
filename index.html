Good questions

Is this a product?

No. UTXOAGENT is an independent community experiment exploring ideas, not a finished service.

Does it promise future features?

No. Everything here is exploratory and may change or never happen.

Why use Bitcoin for names?

Names anchored to Bitcoin are scarce and don't depend on a single company's account system.

Write to us. We are happy to talk with anyone curious about Realms and agents.
