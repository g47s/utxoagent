01 / The foundation

A name anchored
to Bitcoin.

Atomicals is a protocol for creating and managing digital objects directly on Bitcoin. Realms bring names into that world: scarce, human-readable identifiers that can serve as personal identities, community roots, or starting points for new experiments.

01 — IDENTITY

Names with roots

A Realm can represent an identity or a shared point of reference, tied to Bitcoin rather than a conventional account database.

02 — COMPOSABILITY

Worlds within worlds

Names, subrealms, containers and digital objects invite new ways to organize communities and create connected experiences.

03 — EXPERIMENTATION

Room to build

The interesting question is what people will build on top of these primitives: tools, marketplaces, identity systems, and more.

02 / The possibility

From Realm
to agent.

Imagine a future in which a Realm can become the public identity of an autonomous software agent: discoverable by name, able to coordinate tasks, and connected to Bitcoin-native assets.

Built on Bitcoin.
Designed to explore.
