Worked example: the root Realm +bitagent

Illustrative structure, in English. Descriptions are suggestions, not protocol rules.

One root name, five branches, eighteen subrealms. Each subrealm is a unit that could have its own keys, rules and agent, as argued in the thesis.

+bitagent

Branch 1: Governance

  • bitagent.governance.councilSets direction and approves major decisions
  • bitagent.governance.proposalsCollects and publishes proposals
  • bitagent.governance.votingRuns votes and records results
  • bitagent.governance.legalsTracks legal and compliance matters

Branch 2: Finance

  • bitagent.finance.treasuryHolds and moves the organization’s funds
  • bitagent.finance.auditingReviews flows and checks records
  • bitagent.finance.budgetingPlans and limits spending

Branch 3: Operations

  • bitagent.operations.executorCarries out approved actions
  • bitagent.operations.solverFinds ways to complete tasks
  • bitagent.operations.resourcesManages tools and assets

Branch 4: Technology

  • bitagent.technology.developmentBuilds and maintains the software
  • bitagent.technology.indexersReads and serves on-chain data
  • bitagent.technology.securityGuards keys and systems

Branch 5: Community

  • bitagent.community.supportHelps users and members
  • bitagent.community.marketingTells the story outside
  • bitagent.community.moderationKeeps spaces healthy

Safeguards to consider for a real deployment: multi-signature control, spending limits, human review of large actions and regular audits.
